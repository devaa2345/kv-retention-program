# Addendum to PREREG.md — 2026-09-03

Written after Phase 1 was already ~3.5 hours into its run (seed ~3032/150 at time of
writing). Phase 1 was **not** restarted or modified for this — see §4. This addendum
does not change PREREG.md; it is a separate, separately-hashed document that narrows
how PREREG.md's budget sweep may be read.

## 1. The defect, named plainly

**G3 (and G1) were validated during Phase 0-R calibration at a single budget (280
tokens). Phase 1 then swept 5 budgets (51/103/154/257/514 tokens, derived as fractions
of mean context length per PREREG.md §2) without re-deriving each budget's competitive
room against the calibrated gates before launch.** The gate itself was correct and the
check was cheap and available at design time — `competitive(B) = B − recency_window(64)
− sink(1)` — it simply wasn't applied per cell before Phase 1 started.

## 2. Verified consequence: B=51 is a budget-honoring violation, not only a headroom problem

`engine.py`'s `_run_select` reserves a foundational sink+recency window unconditionally,
before any policy gets to compete for budget:

```
retained_cap(B) = foundational + max(0, B − foundational),  foundational = 65
```

| budget | foundational | competitive (B−65) | retained_cap | nominal honored? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 51 | 65 | **−14** | **65** | **NO — retains 65 vs nominal 51 (+27%)** |
| 103 | 65 | 38 | 103 | yes (exact) |
| 154 | 65 | 89 | 154 | yes (exact) |
| 257 | 65 | 192 | 257 | yes (exact) |
| 514 | 65 | 449 | 514 | yes (exact) |

This is the same class of defect as the original single-credential pilot's
structural-protection budget overrun (a reservation mechanism outrunning its budget
parameter) — triggered this time by the recency floor rather than protection, and
confirmed both analytically (table above, precomputable before running anything) and
empirically, from Phase 1's own logged data at time of writing:

| arm | mean @ B=51 (n=70, oracle n=35) | mean @ B=103 |
|---|---|---|
| no_protect_permanent | 0.014 | 0.014 |
| protect_permanent | 0.014 | 0.081 |
| recoverable_no_protection | 0.014 | 0.014 |
| recoverable_protected | 0.017 | 0.131 |
| oracle_static | 0.014 | 0.267 |

At B=51 every arm collapses to the *same* ~0.014 floor, statistically
indistinguishable from one another — because none of them receive any competitive
budget at all, so no policy (protection, recoverability, or oracle knowledge) can
express a decision. At B=103 the arms differentiate cleanly (protection lifts
0.014→0.081, recoverable+protection 0.014→0.131, oracle 0.014→0.267), consistent with
the budget being honored but the oracle ceiling (0.267) still far below G1 (≥0.95).

## 3. Cell classification for the primary analysis

| budget | competitive room | budget honored | oracle (G1 check) | status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 51 | −14 | **NO** | 0.015 | **VOID** — budget-honoring violation; no arm can express a policy decision |
| 103 | 38 | yes | 0.265 | **VOID** — budget honored, but insufficient headroom for oracle to represent an achievable ceiling (G1 fails badly) |
| 154 | 89 | yes | 0.621 | **PARTIAL** — real dynamic range exists (arms differentiate), but G1 fails (0.621 < 0.95); usable on its own terms, not poolable with 257/514 as an equivalent ceiling |
| 257 | 192 | yes | 0.955 | **VALID** — G1 passes, matches calibration (0.983 at the single calibration budget, 280) closely |
| 514 | 449 | yes | 0.975 | **VALID** — G1 passes |

The exclusion criterion is arithmetic (competitive room, computable at design time), not
outcome-dependent — cells are not excluded because their numbers were inconvenient.

## 4. Why Phase 1 was left running, not patched or restarted

Patching `recency_window` (or any nuisance parameter) mid-run would make every arm at
every budget two different experiments pooled into one table for seeds before vs. after
the patch — the paired, prompt-clustered design in PREREG.md §3 depends on every prompt
seeing the *same* fixed policy definition across the whole run. Restarting would discard
~3.5 hours of a run whose only defect is confined to 1-2 of 5 budget points, for a
one-line parameter that doesn't need changing anywhere else. Cheaper and more honest to
exclude the affected cells at analysis time than to break the design to save them.

## 5. Consequence for PREREG.md §3's "no functional form assumed across budgets"

That commitment stands unchanged — the 5 budget points are still reported independently,
no curve fit through them. What narrows is the range those points can honestly speak to:
only 257 and 514 clear every calibration gate; 154 is usable but explicitly not an
equivalent ceiling to the other two (its own oracle sits at 62% of achievable, not
~95%+); 51 and 103 are excluded from the primary interaction estimand. Any statement
about how the protection × recoverability interaction moves with budget is therefore a
claim resting on **two clean points (257, 514) plus one qualified point (154)** — not a
five-point gradient across the originally intended 5%-50% range. This will be stated
explicitly wherever Phase 1 results are reported.

## 6. Disposition: report, don't delete

`results/phase1/raw_results.jsonl` retains every row for all 5 budgets — nothing is
removed from the dataset. The (not yet written) Phase 1 analysis script will report all
5 budgets in one table, each row annotated with its competitive-room and
void/partial/valid status from §3 above, and will compute the primary interaction
estimand only over {154 (qualified), 257, 514}, with {51, 103} excluded and the
arithmetic from §2 given as the stated reason, not silently dropped.

## 7. Carried forward from PREREG.md, noted here for a single point of reference

`quant_byte_cost = 0.25` (the iso-memory accounting assumption — `engine.py` does not
actually store QUANT-tier tokens in a smaller dtype, so iso-token/iso-memory parity is
enforced by an assumed relative cost, not measured GPU bytes) is unchanged from
PREREG.md §2/§3 and is not affected by this addendum. Noted here only so both caveats
are visible from one place.

## 9. Correction (same day, later) — `iso_condition` pooling bug in interim analysis, not the data

Every interim analysis query run against `results/phase1/raw_results.jsonl` during live
monitoring today, up to this point, filtered rows by `budget` alone for arms 3 and 4
(`3_recoverable_no_protection`, `4_recoverable_protected`). `run_phase1.py` logs the
*nominal* budget in the `budget` field for every row regardless of `iso_condition`, but
for the tiered arms the `iso_memory` condition actually runs at a **different, larger**
token count (`round(nominal_budget / 0.625)`, ≈1.6× nominal — see `iso_memory_budget_tokens`
in `run_phase1.py`, PREREG.md §2's iso-memory derivation). That conversion was never
written to its own field in the row. The result: every interim number reported for arms 3
and 4 today was an unweighted pool of the true-nominal-budget run and the
true-1.6×-nominal-budget run under one label. Arms 1, 2, 5, 6 are unaffected — they are
not tiered, so `budget` is unambiguous for them regardless of `iso_condition`.

**Corrected reads, `iso_token` only (matched raw token count — the fair comparison to
arms 1/2/5/6 at the same nominal budget):**

| budget | recoverable alone | protection alone | protection+recovery | oracle | raw interaction | normalized |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 154 | 0.012 | 0.185 | 0.189 | 0.628 | +0.004 | +0.009 |
| 257 | 0.008 | 0.409 | 0.414 | 0.934 | +0.004 | +0.008 |
| 514 | 0.010 | 0.963 | 0.958 | 0.956 | −0.004 | (headroom≈0, degenerate) |

Under true matched-token-count comparison, **the interaction is ~0 at every budget** —
recoverable tiering adds nothing beyond protection alone when both arms hold the same
number of retained tokens. This is a sharper, cleaner version of H-SUB than anything
reported earlier today: not "the interaction is present but shrinks," but "the interaction
is absent under the comparison that actually isolates the mechanism."

**For reference, `iso_memory` only (arms 3/4 running at ≈1.6× the raw token count, same
accounted byte cost as the nominal-budget permanent arms):**

| nominal budget | true token budget | recoverable alone | protection+recovery |
|---|---|---|---|
| 154 | 246 | 0.008 | 0.389 |
| 257 | 411 | 0.008 | 0.952 |
| 514 | 822 | 0.023 | 0.912 |

The large apparent gains here track having ~60% more raw retained tokens, not a
recoverability mechanism effect — this is exactly the confound PREREG.md §2 designed the
iso-token/iso-memory split to separate, and separating them correctly (rather than pooling
both under one nominal-budget label, as every interim report today did before this
correction) is what makes the null result under iso-token legible instead of masked by an
unweighted average of a real effect and a token-count confound.

**Disposition:** no data was lost or needs to be rerun — `budget` and `iso_condition` are
both already recorded on every row; this was purely an analysis-script defect, not an
instrument or data defect, and the actual token budget for any `iso_memory` row is exactly
recoverable as `round(budget / 0.625)`. All figures reported live in conversation for arms
3/4 prior to this section should be treated as superseded by the table above, not as
additional evidence — they were the same underlying rows, incorrectly pooled.

## 10. `quant_byte_cost` sensitivity (2026-09-04) — the iso-memory dissociation does not depend on it

`quant_byte_cost = 0.25` was chosen after PREREG.md was hashed and sets the iso-memory
token multiplier (`T = B / (0.5 + 0.5·quant_byte_cost)`; 1.60× at 0.25, 1.48× at 0.30).
Because the Phase 1 headline is a *dissociation* between iso-token (null) and iso-memory
(large positive), and only the iso-memory side depends on this constant, it is
load-bearing and was swept explicitly rather than asserted.

One cell: budget 257, arm `4_recoverable_protected` (protection + recoverable tiering),
n=150, seeds 3000–3149, all other parameters identical to Phase 1. Baseline for the
interaction is Phase 1's protection-alone arm at nominal budget 257 (mean 0.423), which is
byte-cost-matched to every row below by construction.
(`kvcache_harness/run_quant_sensitivity.py`, `results/quant_sensitivity/raw_results.jsonl`.)

| quant_byte_cost | multiplier | true token budget | protect+recover | interaction | 95% CI (mean, 10k bootstrap) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.125 | 1.78× | 457 | 0.967 | +0.543 | [+0.518, +0.570] |
| **0.25** (hashed) | 1.60× | 411 | 0.956 | +0.532 | [+0.506, +0.559] |
| 0.30 | 1.48× | 395 | 0.959 | +0.536 | [+0.510, +0.561] |
| 0.50 | 1.33× | 343 | 0.778 | +0.354 | [+0.331, +0.379] |

The dissociation survives the whole plausible range. At 0.30 specifically the interaction
is +0.536 versus +0.532 at 0.25 — indistinguishable. Even at 0.50 (a deliberately
conservative choice implying only 2× compression, where int8-vs-bf16 storage is genuinely
4×) the iso-memory interaction is +0.354 with a CI nowhere near the ±0.05 equivalence
band, while iso-token remains null. So the qualitative claim — *recoverability is inert
under matched tokens and only appears to help when the accounting hands it more tokens* —
is not an artifact of the post-hash constant. The magnitude of the iso-memory effect does
move with `quant_byte_cost` (0.35 to 0.54 across the swept range) and should be reported
as a range with the constant stated, not as a single number.

## 11. Phase 2 halted (2026-09-04): the quantized tier is a no-op at 8-bit, so H-ORTH is untestable there

Phase 2 interim (39/150 prompts) showed all five promotion signals landing on the
protection-alone baseline, *including the oracle* (P5 0.417 vs baseline 0.423, no better
than random's 0.406). An oracle performing no better than random is usually a wiring bug,
so it was checked before being believed (`kvcache_harness/debug_p5_and_quant.py`).

**P5 is correctly wired.** Measured behaviourally — the fraction of credential tokens
ending the run in each tier, budget 257, n=10:

| signal | credential tokens FULL | QUANT | evicted | accuracy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P5 oracle | 0.477 | 0.000 | 0.523 | 0.467 |
| P3 random | 0.214 | 0.263 | 0.523 | 0.417 |
| P1 attention | 0.174 | 0.303 | 0.523 | 0.467 |

The oracle places *every* retained credential token at full precision, 2.7× more than the
attention signal does. Its information reaches the policy and changes behaviour
dramatically. It just does not change the outcome.

**The reason: at 8-bit the FULL/QUANT distinction has no effect on quality.** Holding
retention fixed and varying only precision (n=10, budget 257): all-FULL 0.450, half/half
0.467, all-QUANT 0.467. Quantization is genuinely applied (verified numerically: ~0.95%
relative L2 error on realistic KV vectors, tensors provably differ — not a pass-through
bug). It is simply too mild to matter for this task.

**Precision sweep** (`kvcache_harness/debug_precision_sweep.py`, n=20, budget 257,
retention held identical, all-FULL vs all-QUANT):

| tier bit-width | relative L2 error | all-FULL | all-QUANT | gap |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | 0.95% | 0.383 | 0.383 | +0.000 |
| 4 | 11.9% | 0.383 | 0.158 | +0.225 |
| 3 | 24.9% | 0.383 | 0.000 | +0.383 |
| 2 | 54.4% | 0.383 | 0.000 | +0.383 |

**Consequences.** (a) H-ORTH is *untestable* at 8-bit rather than false: the dependent
variable the promotion signal controls has no effect on the outcome, so P1≈P2≈P3≈P4≈P5 is
forced by construction, not evidence about signal orthogonality. Phase 2 was stopped at
395 rows / 39 prompts (resumable, `results/phase2/raw_results.jsonl` retained) rather than
spend a further ~2.5 GPU-hours confirming a degenerate measurement. (b) 4-bit is the only
swept precision where the promotion decision can carry information — QUANT tokens are
degraded but not destroyed. 3-bit and below destroy them outright, which is again a regime
where no promotion policy helps. (c) This converges with Phase 1 from a second direction:
Phase 1 found recoverability inert at matched tokens and helpful only when the accounting
bought it more tokens; this finds the tier's precision dimension carries no quality signal
at deployable 8-bit at all. Together: **at int8, a quantized recoverable tier's entire
value is byte-efficiency of retention, and the promotion/reversibility logic is
decoration — it only has a job in precision regimes where the tier is itself damaging the
cache.**

`engine.QUANT_BITS` now exposes the tier's bit-width (default 8, unchanged for everything
reported above) so this is a measurable parameter rather than a hardcoded assumption.

## 12. Phase 2 result (2026-09-04): H-ORTH falsified — the promotion decision is saturated

Re-run at 4-bit, the only swept precision where the tier is damaging but not destructive
(§11). Protection ON and recoverable tiering ON in every arm; eviction attention-ranked
throughout; the promotion signal is the sole varied factor
(`kvcache_harness/run_phase2.py`, `cache/protected_split_tier.py`,
`results/phase2_4bit/raw_results.jsonl`, n=150, seeds 3000–3149).

**Budget 257** (usable band at 4-bit: all-QUANT 0.133 → all-FULL 0.411):

| signal | ρ(eviction, promotion) | mean | vs P1 | 95% CI (mean paired diff, 10k bootstrap) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P1 attention (reference) | **+1.000** | 0.203 | — | — |
| P2 epiphany | −0.077 | 0.216 | +0.012 | [−0.016, +0.039] |
| P3 random | −0.050 | 0.154 | **−0.049** | **[−0.077, −0.021]** |
| P4 roundrobin | +0.002 | 0.184 | −0.019 | [−0.047, +0.009] |
| P5 oracle (future need) | −0.068 | 0.188 | −0.016 | [−0.046, +0.013] |

**Budget 154** (band 0.056 → 0.167, marginal — reported separately, not pooled): same
ordering, all effects compressed, nothing clears zero including P3 (−0.013,
[−0.030, +0.003]).

**H-ORTH is falsified, not merely unsupported.** The manipulation worked — ρ is exactly
+1.000 for the circular arm and ≈0 for the others — and P2, the orthogonal candidate, does
not beat P1: +0.012 with a CI spanning zero. Circularity between the eviction and
promotion signals was not what made recoverability inert. Unlike Phase 1's null, the arms
do disagree prompt-by-prompt (55–95 of 150 prompts differ), so this is genuine equivalence
in expectation rather than identical behaviour.

P3 (random) is the only arm reliably below P1, so the promotion signal is not ignored —
it is **saturated**: any non-degenerate ordering performs equally, and only actively bad
ordering costs anything. P4 (rotation) ties P1, so the blind-rotation-beats-measured-ranking
result from the adjacent block-diffusion work does **not** reproduce in this architecture.

**P5 is a genuine ceiling, verified rather than assumed.** P5 landing marginally *below*
P1 prompted a check that it was not "oracle on the subset that fits"
(`kvcache_harness/debug_p5_oversubscription.py`, budget 257, 4-bit, n=12): the future-need
set is 111 tokens, FULL slots are 96, but only ~53 of those tokens survive retention, and
**0.000 of selection calls were oversubscribed** — P5 promotes every retained credential
token, with zero ending in QUANT. So "even a perfect promotion signal buys nothing" is
supported. The P5-below-P1 inversion is noise (CI [−0.046, +0.013]).

**Scope line, important:** P5 is a ceiling *for the promotion decision only*, not for the
policy. It cannot rescue the 52.3% of credential tokens evicted at the retention stage,
because retention stays attention-ranked in every arm by design — that is what isolates
the promotion signal as the sole varied factor. Phase 1's `oracle_static` shows what
perfect *retention* buys at the same budget: 0.943 versus protection-alone's 0.423.

**Composite claim across Phases 1 and 2:** the promotion decision is information-saturated
— a perfect signal adds nothing — while retention carries ~0.52 of unexploited headroom at
the same budget. The recoverable tier's machinery is aimed at the dimension that does not
matter. Per PREREG.md §4's kill gate this is the "P1 ≈ P2 ≈ P5" branch: no promotion
signal helps, so Phases 3–6 are not worth GPU time beyond what supports the negative claim.

**Quantizer scope.** All of §11–§12 holds for *this* quantizer (per-position affine
min–max over the (kv_heads, head_dim) slice) at these bit-widths. A scheme with different
group sizes or per-channel scales — QEvict's included — will move the free/damaging
boundary. The structural claim survives that (there exists a precision above which
promotion logic is inert, and int8 is above it here); the boundary's specific location is
implementation-dependent and should be stated as such.

## 13. Level-occupancy check (2026-09-04) — the last standing anomaly hypothesis, ruled out

Verified level *counts* (§11–12) say nothing about how mass is *distributed* across those
levels. Under per-position affine min–max quantization an outlier pushes most of a vector's
mass into a few central levels, so effective resolution can sit well below nominal. If 5-
and 6-bit concentrated mass into few levels while 4-bit spread across its own, that would
explain a non-monotone accuracy curve with no bug anywhere.

Measured on real cached K/V (`kvcache_harness/debug_level_occupancy.py`, 3 prompts × 28
layers). Effective level count is `2^H`, H = Shannon entropy of the level-occupancy
distribution:

| bits | nominal | effective K | effective V | ratio K | ratio V | top-1 level share (K) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | 256 | 97.6 | 156.8 | 0.381 | 0.612 | 3.7% |
| 7 | 128 | 48.9 | 78.5 | 0.382 | 0.613 | 6.5% |
| 6 | 64 | 24.4 | 39.1 | 0.382 | 0.612 | 12.7% |
| 5 | 32 | 12.2 | 19.4 | 0.381 | 0.606 | 20.5% |
| 4 | 16 | 6.1 | 9.5 | 0.383 | 0.593 | 33.3% |
| 3 | 8 | 3.1 | 4.6 | 0.391 | 0.572 | 56.2% |

The occupancy ratio is constant to three decimals across every width, and effective levels
halve exactly in step with nominal. The distribution's shape is scale-invariant in
bit-width; 5- and 6-bit do not concentrate mass relative to 4-bit. **Ruled out.**

The instrument has now been cleared five independent ways: level counts on live intercepted
calls; reconstruction error monotone through the live path with no subnormal or non-finite
values; identical policy behaviour across widths; collapse near-universal rather than a
per-prompt threshold; and constant level occupancy. The non-monotonicity stands as a
characterised, reproducible anomaly with no established mechanism, and is reported as such
rather than attached to a sixth story.
