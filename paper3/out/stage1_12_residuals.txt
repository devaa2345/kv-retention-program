================================================================================================
READING
================================================================================================
The residual is not noise. It is a monotone function of budget with r ~ +0.88, it
changes sign exactly at the calibration budget, and it does so in all four arms on
both models. `c_eff` in F2 and F3 depends only on c and rho, both held fixed across
budgets; the DATA's implied c_eff (ln q / ln p_g, tabulated in test 1.1) rises
monotonically from ~1.10 at C=16 to ~2.5-3.2 at C=512. The single fitted parameter
is absorbing a budget dependence the functional form does not contain.

Consequence for 1.3, and it is not a coincidence: the crossover test is evaluated at
the top budget, which is where the over-prediction is largest. keydiff's completion
is over-predicted by a factor of ~8 at M3 C=512 (residual +0.907), which is what puts
it into the predicted crossover set and keeps the two arms that actually win out of
it. 1.3 does not fail for an independent reason; it fails because the form is wrong
in a direction that is worst exactly where 1.3 looks.
