the queried record is available SOMEWHERE across the 224 slots for essentially every

  floor_pos         0.2700       0.2700        0.2700      +0.0000
  snapkv            0.1388       0.9850        0.1838      -0.8462
  expected_attn     0.3463       0.9200        0.0780      -0.5737
  keydiff           0.2075       0.3362        0.0462      -0.1288
  adakv_snapkv      0.3962       1.0000        0.2007      -0.6038

So the earlier reading -- that expected_attn answers more often than the record is
available -- was an artefact of the LINE unit being too strict, and does not survive.
There is no accuracy-above-availability anomaly here and no leak is implied.

What is left is the real finding and it cuts both ways:
  * Union availability at this cell is ~1.0 for snapkv, expected_attn and
    adakv_snapkv, while their accuracies span 0.139 to 0.396 and the floor sits at
    0.270. Availability somewhere across heads is close to uninformative here --
    which is exactly T3's claim, and it is why the union measure inverts.
  * Per-head availability is far closer to accuracy in level, and across all 44
    cells it contradicts accuracy 4.5% of the time against the union's 38.6%.
  * But at THIS cell neither measure ranks the four methods: rho_s is 0.10 for
    LINE q_mean and 0.30 for the other three. Per-head fixes the LEVEL of the
    snapkv inversion without explaining the ORDER of the four arms. Stage 1 should
    not be reported as having resolved M3 C=512; it has resolved one specific
    contradiction inside it.
