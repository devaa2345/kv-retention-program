### 1.6 The 91-copy audit, boundary rule, and SQuAD-specific diagnosis

After the lexical result was locked, the CPU-only 91-failure audit (`out/_realtext_pointer_failure_audit.py`, `out/realtext_pointer_91_failure_audit.jsonl`, `REALTEXT_POINTER_M2_RESULT.md`, `090b79c`) classified failures **given gold sentence selected**: **31** disjoint wrong spans (28 SQuAD/3 needle), **30** starts inside gold or clipped boundary (9/21), **20** correct starts stopped early (8/12), **4** copies with extra prefix (2/2), **5** partial overlaps (2/3), **1** correct start copied beyond gold (1/0), **0** empty. The last five non-disjoint categories total **60 boundary/overlap** errors. Needle failures overlapping the right code were **38/41**; SQuAD disjoint wrong spans were **28/50**. The requested “28 different-span” is therefore a subgroup count, not the overall 31. No token continuation can “drift” away from a contiguous literal substring after a fixed start without ending or choosing another occurrence.

The answer-blind code-boundary rule was designed on fresh development data and frozen in `PREREG_REALTEXT_BOUNDARY_M2.md` (`8554ac8`, held-out lock `13b8f20`). It activates for an access-code question with exactly one isolated 4–12-digit run in the selected sentence, permits prefixes of that whole run, and permits EOS only after the full run; otherwise it falls back to unchanged `literal_v1`. Development prefill cost **.74 min**, development generation **1.95 min** after load. ### 1.10 Exact per-query tight-KV design and M2 confirmation

### 1.13 Custom-retention pointer-hit direction and the Phase 1 artifact check

The latest request switched from raw accuracy to **pointer hit-rate at the same floor token budget**. Prior retention-side searches (floor, SnapKV/U-X, floor-anchored hybrid, ratio sweeps, MMR/coverage) had not beaten floor on their raw-accuracy targets; the focused mechanism instead requires that the pointer find the queried unit in the kept evidence. The proposed exploratory phases were: reuse MMR/floor sets and frozen pointer with no generation; if negative or inconclusive, design one relevance-free, whole-unit diversity policy after a ceiling; then gate any small 5070 accuracy test on a positive pointer-hit CI. No 8B/48GB work was authorized.

The **Phase 1 artifact check did not yield a hit-rate**. `out/_lever2_M2.jsonl` contains **986 rows = 493 paired floor_pos + 493 MMR-SnapKV**, but its fields contain aggregate `p_g`, completion, generated text and score, **no per-head keeps, gather orders, attention arrays, pointer selections or pointer hits**. `out/_item4_maxattn.py` rebuilds floor/oracle, not MMR. Treating a generated answer as the pointer would change the estimand. No model was loaded and no new result was saved. Phase 1 is **not measured**, not a null CI or evidence against MMR. A new prefill-only capture driver would violate the specific “no new code” Phase 1 condition unless separately authorized. Phase 2 and Phase 3 have **not started**; the exploratory track is not a final preregistration, result or 8B gate.

## 4. Standing experimental and reporting rules

The standing rule for a confirmatory comparison is to decide the arm definitions, prompt, scoring, gate, sample, allocation and analysis **before generating the held-out answers**; save and hash the manifest and freeze the code. When a pilot exposed a prompt-format failure, Amendment 1 was written and committed before compressed generation; the original anchor failure and its classification stayed in the record. The later 128-token matched-memory control did not silently become a win over plain floor when its CI touched zero. A development screen, an exploratory artifact and a confirmatory result remain separate even when their outcomes agree. Sources: `PREREG_REALTEXT_5070.md`, `PREREG_REALTEXT_TIGHT_KV_M2_M3.md`, `REALTEXT_FOCUS_M3_ARTIFACT_AUDIT.md`.

Before generation, measure how often the gold unit is **whole-retained** and how often the frozen pointer selects it, with denominators reported as unconditional queries and, where useful, conditional on retention. These are upper bounds on a pointer-plus-extractor arm, not its predicted accuracy. The floor held-but-wrong gate required observed generation; those cells were deferred rather than inferred from prefill, and its 3/96 result stopped a floor masked arm. The oracle-sentence or oracle-span arm is a ceiling and must never be labelled deployable. At a given retention budget, count actual tokenized KV use per query, not just a nominal fraction; for tight KV, include the second focused prefill in the equality accounting. Sources: `PREREG_REALTEXT_5070.md`, `REALTEXT_5070_M2_RESULT.md`, `PREREG_REALTEXT_TIGHT_KV_M2_M3.md`.

Accuracy is strict whole-answer exact match after casefold and whitespace-run normalization; finding the gold string **inside a longer output does not score correct**. `literal_v1` independently enforces that each decoded prefix is a contiguous, case- and whitespace-sensitive substring of the selected span; EOS is legal only for a nonempty legal substring and there is no permissive formatting/restart token. Mask fidelity was CPU-tested on real spans before generation. Report wrong-answer categories, fixed and broken previously correct answers, cap stops and whether generated values occur anywhere in the full context. The corrected invented-value check is against full context, not gold-only text. Sources: `PREREG_REALTEXT_5070.md`, `out/_realtext_5070_mask_test.py`, `CONFIRMATORY_RESULTS.md`.

Check confounds **in both directions**: focused versus unmasked versions of the *same* retention arm must have identical kept-token hashes and budgets, and different retention arms must be reported as different even when their scores match. Verify gold-held and selected-gold flags against raw keep/pointer traces, as well as output-substring validity. If an item cannot be measured from the saved traces, state **not measurable**; do not fill it with a spill, proxy or another arm's value. The MMR pointer-hit request is the current example (§1.13). The M3 pre-clean focus files were reviewed for contamination and explicitly barred from confirmation despite apparently favorable scores. Sources: `REALTEXT_5070_M2_RESULT.md`, `REALTEXT_FOCUS_M3_ARTIFACT_AUDIT.md` and §1.13.

For every real-text paired CI, resample **instances**, carrying their two SQuAD and two needle queries together; report task splits and do not compare floor counts from separate fresh samples as a paired effect. Distinguish the mask's gain over its own unmasked baseline from a whole-system gain over floor, and distinguish focused floor from plain floor. Do not move the decision threshold after reading results, convert a CI touching zero into a win, or treat repeated tuning on the same held-out set as fresh confirmation. Missing/partial generation is not an incorrect answer: the M3 LEDGER run scored only 70 complete instances after excluding partial instance 176. Sources: `CONFIRMATORY_RESULTS.md`, the real-text `PREREG` and `RESULT` files.

Small screens are diagnostic **only**.
