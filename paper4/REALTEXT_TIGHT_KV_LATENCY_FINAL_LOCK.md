# Corrected pilot gate and final timing lock

The original prospective cost definitions remain in `PREREG_REALTEXT_TIGHT_KV_LATENCY.md`. Instrumentation-only commit `0e1ae9b` added a directly persisted raw shared-prefix CUDA event, a separately persisted A1 query-forward event, and a PowerShell outer clock starting before WSL launch. The frozen accuracy method and results were untouched. The identical one-warmup/eight-measured-instance pilot manifest (SHA-256 `0846173aff61931f23cf07796765e308f8d006dd9b890f6656d873aeec86c34a`) was remeasured on M2 and M3 with all three arms.

Mean raw shared-prefix CUDA event for focused SnapKV was **0.3672 s M2** and **0.3851 s M3**. The old `2 × amortized first-event` proxy would have been 0.4358 and 0.4566 s on the corrected traces, respectively, about 19% higher because it includes both A1 forwards. Mean second-pass CUDA events were 0.4027 and 0.4142 s, giving corrected second/raw ratios **1.097 M2** and **1.075 M3**. Focused floor was about 1.098 M2 and 1.076 M3. Both models pass the preregistered 0.5–2 sanity gate. Pointer time remained far below second-prefill time.

Corrected warm per-query means for plain floor / focused floor / focused SnapKV were M2 **0.2883 / 0.7273 / 0.7175 s** and M3 **0.4109 / 0.8090 / 0.7249 s**. These phase and total means are close to the original pilot readout; no arm's phase mean falls outside its earlier per-query range. Individual extremes did cross old ranges: for example M2 focused-floor second prefill reached 0.479 s versus the old 0.462 s maximum, M2 plain-floor generation reached 0.178 s versus 0.142 s, and M3 focused-floor pointer reached 0.072 s versus 0.062 s. Treat these as observed timing spread, not as identical traces.

Outer wall, measured before WSL launch, was **60.144 s M2** and **59.677 s M3**. The WSL-to-Python-process-start intervals were 1.521 and 0.076 s. Python job wall was 52.469 and 56.108 s; the outer-minus-Python-job remainders were 7.675 and 3.569 s, including the WSL interval, Python import/setup before the in-Python timer, and shutdown. Model load inside the Python job was 19.690 and 19.745 s; warmup was 4.640 and 4.789 s. These setup times are disclosed separately and are not amortized into the primary warm-query ratio.

Using the frozen formula and corrected eight-instance paired variances, the four required instance counts (M2 focused floor, M2 focused SnapKV, M3 focused floor, M3 focused SnapKV, each minus plain floor) are **11, 32, 48, 24**. The maximum **N=48** replaces the original provisional 78 because the corrected pilot's variance estimate changed materially. This is a timing precision design, not an accuracy design.
