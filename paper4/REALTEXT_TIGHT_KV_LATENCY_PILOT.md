# Tight-KV latency pilot: diagnostic readout

The timing-only pilot used one warm-up instance (1218) and eight measured instances (1223, 1225, 1227, 1230, 1231, 1235, 1236, 1239), two SQuAD queries each, identically across all three arms and both models. Manifest SHA-256: `0846173aff61931f23cf07796765e308f8d006dd9b890f6656d873aeec86c34a`. The prior accuracy sets were excluded by question. No accuracy score was calculated or frozen accuracy result changed. All 48 arm-query records completed per model. Times below are seconds per query, with the first shared context prefill divided equally between its two questions. Entries are **mean ± sample SD** across 16 queries; ranges follow in brackets. These are pilot estimates, not final cost claims.

CUDA-event means for first and second prefill were respectively M2 .220/.402 seconds for focused SnapKV and M3 .234/.423 seconds. Because the first prefill is shared, the diagnostic proxy `second event / (2 × amortized first event)` is .91 for M2 and .90 for M3, within the preregistered 0.5–2 range. The focused-floor values are also about .91. The second prefill dominates the added work; neither pointer is unexpectedly slower than it. Pilot focused SnapKV warm wall was +.433 seconds/query (2.46× plain floor) for M2 and +.319 seconds/query (1.77×) for M3. Generation lengths differ among arms, especially on M3, so these ratios are observed end-to-end query latencies rather than a pure second-pass microbenchmark.

The measured post-model-load processing totals for all three arms together were 28.99 seconds M2 and 31.91 seconds M3. Python-process wall totals were 52.26 and 56.37 seconds; these included model load (18.24 and 19.58 seconds) and warm-up (5.03 and 4.88 seconds). **Instrumentation gap:** the outer clock started inside Python, after WSL launch, so those totals do not capture WSL startup. Also, the raw shared-prefix CUDA event was not persisted separately; the sanity check above uses twice the amortized first-event total, which includes two A1 query forwards and is only a proxy for one raw context prefill. Both clocks must be recorded directly in the full-run harness. The observed phase magnitudes are plausible, but the accounting gate is not fully closed until these two instrumentation gaps are fixed and checked.

Pilot instance-paired SDs of total warm-wall differences were M2 .042 seconds for focused floor vs plain floor and .093 for focused SnapKV vs plain floor; M3 .102 and .065 seconds. Under the frozen 10%-of-plain-floor timing threshold, the four required sizes are 16, 78, 47, and 19 instances. Their maximum is **78 instances**, above the 32-instance minimum and below the 160-instance stop branch. This is a provisional final N, **not authorization to run**: first correct and verify the two timing-accounting gaps, then lock the fresh final manifest. No final timing run was started.
