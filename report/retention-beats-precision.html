Retention Beats Precision

A recoverable quantized KV cache tier was tested against the mechanism it claims to provide. Across two independent routes, the promotion machinery does nothing: at matched token count it adds zero, and at deployable int8 the tier it promotes into carries no quality signal at all. What the cache actually needs is decided one stage earlier.

Finding 1 · Phase 1
Recoverability is inert at matched tokens

The protection × recoverability interaction is null at every budget under iso-token accounting (CI within ±0.05), and large under iso-memory — where the tiered arm is quietly handed ~1.6× the tokens. The dissociation is the result.

Finding 2 · Phase 2
No promotion signal helps, including a perfect one

Five promotion signals from perfectly circular (ρ = +1.000) to orthogonal to oracular land within 0.03 of each other. Only a deliberately random ordering is measurably worse. H-ORTH falsified.

Finding 3 · Diagnostic
The binding constraint is retention

52.3% of credential tokens are evicted before promotion ever runs. A perfect promotion oracle scores 0.188; a perfect retention oracle scores 0.943 at the same budget.

01

What was under test

A line of work fixes the irreversibility of autoregressive KV eviction by adding a recoverable middle tier: high-confidence windows stay at full precision, intermediate windows are quantized but retained and can be promoted back, and only the lowest tier is deleted. A parallel line fixes the same failure structurally, by reserving cache for boundary or pattern-matched spans regardless of attention score. Neither line tests the other.

Two hypotheses were pre-registered. H-SUB: protection and recoverability address the same failure — information becoming unreachable — so once you have either, the other adds nothing. H-COMP: they address different failures, protection handling structurally critical low-attention tokens and recoverability handling drift, predicting a positive interaction. A sharper sub-hypothesis followed from the pilot: H-ORTH — recoverability has value if and only if the promotion signal is statistically independent of the eviction signal, since promoting on the same cumulative-attention score that caused a demotion is circular by construction.

The task is multi-credential retrieval with induced dormancy: six credentials buried in a ~1030-token config dump, queried one per conversational turn in shuffled order, scored as the fraction retrieved (k/6). Query rank is the dormancy manipulation — a credential asked last has had five intervening turns in which its cache entry could be demoted and must be recovered.

02

The instrument, before the experiment

Five calibration gates had to pass before any inferential run. Two of them exist specifically to catch an instrument that cannot express the quantity under test.

A first pilot failed silently on exactly that. Structural protection pinned every credential to full precision unconditionally, so the recoverable tier had nothing to recover, and all four cells of the 2×2 were inert — the factor under test could not move the outcome. The gate that catches this is G3 (headroom): protection alone must land in 0.45–0.75, neither saturating nor collapsing.

03

Phase 1 — the dissociation

The same experiment, scored two ways, gives opposite answers. That is the finding, and it is a claim about how this literature evaluates rather than about any one system.

A recoverable tier retains evicted content in quantized form, so at "matched budget = N retained tokens" it consumes strictly more memory than permanent eviction. Both accountings were run. Under iso-token the arms hold the same number of retained tokens. Under iso-memory they hold the same accounted bytes, which — with a quantized slot costed at 0.25 of a full slot — hands the tiered arm about 1.6× the tokens.

The equivalence is stronger than the intervals suggest, because the arms are not merely close on average — they are frequently identical. Protection and protection-plus-recovery return the same score on 147, 145 and 141 of 150 prompts at 154, 257 and 514 respectively. At 257 only five prompts differ at all: two favour protection, three favour protection-plus-recovery.

A note on the estimator

The pre-registration fixed a bootstrapped median of paired differences. On this data that is the wrong summary and produces degenerate zero-width intervals: the differences are overwhelmingly exactly zero, so the median is zero in essentially every resample. The mean difference with its interval, reported alongside the count of prompts that differ at all, is what the data supports. This is a deviation from the pre-registration and is recorded as one.

04

Phase 2 — H-ORTH falsified

If circularity between the eviction and promotion signals were what made recoverability inert, an orthogonal signal would rescue it. It does not — and neither does knowing the future.

Protection is held on, tiering is held on, eviction stays attention-ranked, and the promotion signal is the only varied factor. Composing the existing protection wrapper with a tiering policy had to be rebuilt first: it pinned protected groups straight to full precision and consumed the entire competitive budget, so nothing ever entered the quantized tier and all five signals returned byte-identical results. Separating retention (protection decides what cannot be evicted) from precision (the promotion signal decides what deserves full fidelity) is both the fix and the more faithful mechanism.

The orthogonal candidate does not beat the perfectly circular one. What separates from the pack is a deliberately uninformative ordering, and it separates downward — so the promotion signal is not ignored, it is saturated. Any non-degenerate ordering performs about equally; only actively bad ordering costs anything. Rotation ties measured ranking, so the blind-rotation-beats-ranking result from the adjacent block-diffusion work does not reproduce in this architecture.

P5 landing marginally below P1 prompted a check that it was not silently "oracle on whatever subset fits". It is not: the future-need set is 111 tokens against 96 full-precision slots, but only ~53 of those tokens survive retention, and 0.000 of selection calls were oversubscribed — every retained credential token is promoted, none end quantized. The inversion is noise.

The promotion decision is information-saturated — a perfect signal adds nothing — while retention carries roughly 0.52 of unexploited headroom at the same budget. The recoverable tier's machinery is aimed at the dimension that does not matter.

P5 bounds the promotion decision only. It cannot rescue the 52.3% of credential tokens evicted at the retention stage, because retention is held attention-ranked in every arm by design — that is what isolates the promotion signal as the sole varied factor. The retention ceiling is oracle_static at the same budget: 0.943 against protection-alone's 0.423.

05

The tier is free, and therefore idle

Phase 2 had to be run twice. At the bit-width everyone actually deploys, the quantity the promotion signal controls has no effect on the outcome at all.

Holding retention fixed and varying only precision at int8: all-full 0.450, half-and-half 0.467, all-quantized 0.467. A quantized credential reads exactly as well as a full-precision one. Quantization is genuinely applied — ~1.2% relative error on real cached tensors, values provably differ — it is simply too mild to matter. That makes P1 ≈ P2 ≈ P3 ≈ P4 ≈ P5 true by construction rather than by evidence, and H-ORTH untestable at int8 rather than false. The first Phase 2 run was stopped at 39 prompts once this was established, and re-run at 4-bit where a usable band was measured to exist.

Which converges with Phase 1

Phase 1 found recoverability inert at matched tokens and useful only when the accounting hands it more. This finds the tier's precision dimension carries no quality signal at deployable precision. Two independent routes, one conclusion: at int8 a recoverable tier's entire value is byte-efficiency of retention, and the promotion logic is decoration — it only has a job in precision regimes where the tier is itself damaging the cache.

06

An unresolved anomaly

Reconstruction error is monotone in bit-width. Task accuracy is not. The instrument has been cleared five ways and the mechanism remains unexplained.

Five hypotheses were tested and all are ruled out. Level counts are correct at every width, asserted on 10,640 quantization calls intercepted during real generation — 4-bit emits at most 16 distinct values, 5-bit 32, 6-bit 62. Scale is correct: relative error measured on those same live calls is perfectly monotone from 1.45% to 37.31%, with no subnormal scales and zero non-finite values, so "correct levels, wrong scale" does not hold. Policy behaviour is identical across widths — same tokens quantized, tier composition matching to three decimals, call counts within 0.4%. The failure is not a per-prompt threshold: at 6 bits, 35 of 50 prompts are fully degenerate and zero are clean, which is near-universal collapse rather than a boundary crossed on some inputs. And level occupancy does not concentrate: the effective level count — 2H of the occupancy distribution — halves exactly in step with the nominal count (97.6, 48.9, 24.4, 12.2, 6.1, 3.1 for keys), holding the occupancy ratio constant to three decimals across every width, so 5- and 6-bit do not crowd their mass into fewer usable levels than 4-bit does.

What is established: empty output is immediate end-of-sequence — a first-step logits failure, tracking the empty rate almost exactly (5-bit 12.5% vs 16.7%; 3-bit 58.3% vs 62.5%). What is not established is why a width carrying four times the error produces four times fewer degenerate outputs. It is reported as a characterised, reproducible anomaly without a mechanism, because the available mechanisms have been tested and falsified.

What this does and does not touch

Phase 2 stands. It is a within-bit-width comparison at a width verified five ways, against a band measured empirically at that width rather than inferred from a position on this curve. All five arms faced identical conditions.

One framing claim dies. "4-bit is the informative regime because it sits between free and destructive" was the premise for re-running Phase 2 there, and it is wrong — the curve is not ordered, so 4-bit sits between nothing. The empirical justification is what actually licensed the run: at 4-bit the band is non-degenerate, measured not inferred, and 4-bit is the one width with a genuinely mixed population.

A methodological caution follows. Retrieval accuracy under KV quantization is not monotone in bit-width even where reconstruction error is. Bit-width sweeps on exact-match tasks cannot be read as severity orderings.

07

Defect ledger

Six defects changed a reported number or invalidated a comparison. Every one was found by a check, not by reading the code — which is the transferable part.

D-01
fp16 degeneracy. Every arm emitted repeated punctuation, including a plain generate() call with no custom code. Qwen2.5 is trained in bf16 and degenerates in fp16 on this stack.
Caught by
reference run
D-02
Protection truncated the value. Sponsorship covered a fixed 12 characters after each label, shorter than the 27-character credential, so the "protected" arm could not see the whole value it was protecting.
Caught by
output inspection
D-03
No recency floor. Pure attention ranking evicted the tokens immediately preceding the answer, breaking local coherence for every arm including the oracle.
Caught by
oracle below ceiling
D-04
Oracle sliced an unordered set. When the must-keep set exceeded budget, an arbitrary, non-reproducible subset of credentials was dropped by hash order. The oracle also lacked credential labels, leaving surviving values unattributable.
Caught by
ceiling shortfall
D-05
Analysis pooled two experiments. The nominal budget was logged identically for both accountings, but the tiered arms run iso-memory at ~1.6× the tokens. Every interim recoverable-arm figure averaged a real effect with a token-count confound.
Caught by
irreproducible query
D-06
The factor under test was inert. Twice — first when protection pinned credentials so tiering had nothing to recover, then again when the 8-bit tier carried no quality signal at all. In both cases every arm returned identical results for a reason that was structural, not empirical.
Caught by
G3 + smoke test

The strongest single check in the project was the cheapest: asserting that the number of distinct values in a quantized tensor is at most 2bits, on the tensors actually used during generation rather than in a standalone test. That form — assert an invariant on live data, not on a reimplementation — would have caught D-04 and D-05 as well. Three of the six defects were surfaced by a reference arm behaving impossibly rather than by any test suite: an oracle below its own ceiling, a policy beating a no-eviction baseline, five arms returning byte-identical results.

08

Scope, and what follows

Everything here holds for one quantizer — per-position affine min–max over the (kv_heads, head_dim) slice — at these bit-widths, one model at 1.5B parameters, and one synthetic task. A scheme with different group sizes or per-channel scales will move the free-versus-damaging boundary. The structural claim survives that: there exists a precision above which promotion logic is inert, and int8 is above it here. The boundary's location is implementation-dependent and should be stated as such.

The budget gradient rests on two clean points and one qualified one, not five. The iso-memory magnitude is a range, not a number. And the pre-registered median estimator was replaced with a mean, which is a recorded deviation rather than a silent one.

Per the pre-registration's own kill gate, this is the branch where no promotion signal helps: later phases are not worth compute beyond what supports the negative claim. The two results that do carry — the iso-token/iso-memory dissociation, and promotion saturation against unexploited retention headroom — are claims about how this literature evaluates recoverable eviction, not about any single system. Both point the same way: if a recoverable tier earns its keep, it earns it by retaining more per byte, and the machinery built to decide what to promote is solving the wrong half of the problem.
